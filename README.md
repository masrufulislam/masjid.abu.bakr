# Masjid Abubakar & Community Center

A digital home for Masjid Abubakar & Community Center in Jamaica, Queens, New York.

## About

The website welcomes visitors to the masjid and highlights the role of prayer, reflection, and community. Its visual identity draws on the serenity of Islamic architecture, warm natural colors, expressive typography, and generous open space.

## Website features

- Responsive layouts for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens
- Light and dark appearance with a remembered theme preference
- Daily calculated salah times and a next-prayer countdown
- Information about the masjid, Jumu’ah, and community life
- Interactive location map, directions, and address copying
- Accessible navigation, keyboard focus indicators, and reduced-motion support

## Prayer time information

Daily prayer times are calculated using the AlAdhan service, the ISNA calculation method, and the Hanafi Asr setting. They are provided as a general reference and may differ from the masjid’s official adhan and iqamah schedule. Visitors should confirm congregational and Friday prayer times directly with the masjid.

## Community and stewardship

The website is designed to make it easier to discover the masjid, plan a visit, and connect with the local Muslim community. Official schedules, announcements, and donation details are subject to confirmation by masjid representatives. The website does not collect donations or personal information.
