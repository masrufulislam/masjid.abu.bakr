Welcome to your masjid

More than a place to pray.

Masjid Abubakar & Community Center is a neighborhood place of worship in Jamaica, Queens. A masjid is more than its walls: it is the people who stand shoulder to shoulder, greet one another with peace, and make space for one another.

Whether you are a familiar face or visiting for the first time, we hope you find a moment of stillness and a sense of belonging.
